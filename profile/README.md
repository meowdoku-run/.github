# Meowdoku — free cat logic puzzle in your browser

**Play: [meowdoku.run](https://meowdoku.run/)** · no download · no account · works on phone, tablet, and desktop

Meowdoku is a cat-themed logic puzzle with the same rules as LinkedIn Queens and Star Battle (one star): place one cat in every row, every column, and every colored region, and never let two cats touch — not even diagonally. There are no numbers and no arithmetic, and every board has exactly one solution you can reach by logic alone.

---

## What you can play

| Mode | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Endless** | Unlimited freshly generated boards in three sizes: Easy 6×6, Normal 7×7, Hard 8×8. Every board is checked for a unique solution the moment it is generated. |
| **Daily** | Three boards a day (Easy, Normal, Hard), identical for everyone, resetting at midnight UTC. |

Every puzzle includes **3 logic hints**. A hint explains the next deduction — which cells a placed cat rules out, or why a region has only one cell left — instead of just revealing the answer. **Undo** takes back moves but never refunds a heart (you get 3).
